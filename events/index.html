Past Editions

2026

Ho Chi Minh City

June 25

On 25 June 2026, in collaboration with Phuong Maxy - Ngonista, Women in Wine & Spirits convened one of the first cross-border initiatives in Vietnam dedicated to women in wine, spirits and hospitality, bringing together professionals from Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, and beyond alongside women producers and leaders from Italy. The evening highlighted the importance of creating spaces where women can exchange experiences, build meaningful connections, and support one another, representing the beginning of a broader effort to strengthen support, visibility and opportunities for women across the Southeast Asia region.

2026

London

April 27

On 27 April, during Real Italian Wine & Food Experience / Wines Experience London, Women in Wine & Spirits hosted “Market Focus | What's Driving the UK Market Today & How Do We Shape What Comes Next?”
Moderated by Lilla O'Connor, the session featured Francesca Mecchia, Roberta Corrà, Emma Hodges, and Christine Barkey, with discussions on UK wine market trends, pricing, education, accessibility, and workplace culture.
The event introduced the Wines Experience “Dialogue in Motion” format and was followed by an evening reception hosted in collaboration with Roberta Corrà - Italia del Vino.

2025

Chicago

October 5

The 2025 Women in wine & Spirits featured a curated roundtable, guided tasting, and networking brunch for over 100 women at RPM Events. After three Chicago editions, our event has become a signature gathering for international buyers, media, and Italian producers.

2024

Chicago

October 20

The Women in Wine & Spirits 2024 brought together an inspiring community of industry professionals at Millennium Hall in Chicago for an evening dedicated to spotlighting women producers in wine and spirits. Through curated tastings, the event highlighted exceptional craftsmanship, quality, and the diverse voices shaping the industry.
